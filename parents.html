For parents

Everything you need to know to set up Chickadoo for your child.

🔐 The Parents' Corner

Tap and hold the gear button for 2 seconds on the home screen to open the Parents' Corner. Young children can't open it by accident. There you can:

  • turn sounds and the voice on or off;
  • choose your child's age (2–3 or 4–5), which changes how hard each game is;
  • pick a play time limit of 10, 20 or 30 minutes;
  • change the language;
  • see stars earned and how often each game was played;
  • turn anonymous crash reports on or off;
  • unlock games or restore earlier purchases.

⏰ Play time limit

When the time is up, Chíp gets sleepy and the app shows a calm bedtime scene. Only a parent can dismiss it (hold the button for 2 seconds). The timer only runs while the app is open.

👑 Free and unlocked content

Every one of the 20 games has a free part your child can play as much as they like, with no time limits and no waiting. The extra levels, songs, animals and scenes of each game are marked with a small lock or a 👑 crown.

You can unlock one game or every game at once. A single game and every game for life are one-time purchases: nothing renews and you keep them forever. Every game for 1 year is an auto-renewing subscription that renews each year until you cancel it, which you can do anytime. Purchases always ask for a parent first.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Sharing and new devices

On iPhone and iPad, purchases work with Family Sharing. On a new device signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account, open Parents' Corner › Unlock games › Restore Purchases.

🗣️ The voice

Chíp speaks using the voices built into your device, so it works offline. If you don't hear the voice, check that it is turned on in the Parents' Corner and that your device has a voice for the selected language (see Support).
