Play, laugh and learn
with Chíp the chick

Chickadoo is a bright little island of 20 games for children aged 2 to 5. Pop bubbles, play the rainbow piano, bake cakes, care for animals and count stars with a friendly chick who talks your child through every step.

🚫

No ads

Never any ads, pop-ups or links that lead out of the app.

✈️

Works offline

Every game plays without the internet, on the road or on a plane.

What your child practises

🖐️

Fine motor skills

Tapping, dragging and drawing with big, forgiving targets.

🔤

Words and sounds

Every instruction is spoken aloud, so no reading is needed.

🔢

Early maths

Counting, numbers, sorting, colors and shapes.

💛

Care and kindness

Feeding, bathing and healing animal friends.

🌏 Speaks 16 languages

Vietnamese, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Thai, Indonesian, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Turkish. The voice follows the language you choose in the Parents' Corner.
