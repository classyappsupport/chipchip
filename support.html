Frequently asked questions

How do I open the Parents' Corner?

On the home screen, press and hold the small gear button in the top corner for 2 seconds, until the circle around it fills up.

Chíp doesn't talk. What can I do?

First check that Voice is on in the Parents' Corner and that the device is not muted. Chíp uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice for the selected language. On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices and download a voice for that language. On Android, go to Settings › System › Languages › Text-to-speech output, choose Speech Services by Google and install the voice data for that language.

How do I unlock a game?

Tap the 👑 crown inside the game, or open Parents' Corner › Unlock games. A parent gate appears first: hold the button for 2 seconds. Then choose that game, or Unlock everything for 1 year or for life. A single game and the lifetime option are one-time purchases. The 1-year plan renews automatically every year and you can cancel it anytime.

How do I cancel the 1-year plan?

On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, choose Chickadoo and cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. On Android, open the Google Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose Chickadoo and cancel. Every game stays unlocked until the end of the year you have paid for. If you switch to the lifetime option, remember to cancel the 1-year plan.

I bought something but it is still locked.

Open Parents' Corner › Unlock games and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so we cannot issue refunds ourselves. On iPhone or iPad, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open the Google Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history, or see Google Play's refund page.

How do I change the language?

Open the Parents' Corner and choose a language under Language. By default the app follows your device language.

How do I set a play time limit?

In the Parents' Corner, under Play time per session, choose 10, 20 or 30 minutes. When the time is up, Chíp goes to sleep and only a parent can continue.

Does Chickadoo need the internet?

No. Every game works offline. The internet is only used to make a purchase and, if you allow it, to send an anonymous crash report.

How do I reset the stars?

Parents' Corner › Achievements › Reset stars.
