Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In short: Chickadoo is made for children aged 2 to 5. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it never asks a child for personal information. Settings and stars are stored only on the device. The only thing that can leave the device is an anonymous crash report, which a parent can turn off.

1. Who we are

Chickadoo (“the app”) is developed by an independent developer (“we”). If you have any question about this policy, write to classyapp.support@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, contacts, precise or approximate location, advertising identifiers (IDFA or Android advertising ID) or any other personal information. The app has no sign-up, no chat, no social features and no user-generated content. It does not use the camera or the microphone.

3. Information stored on the device

To work, the app keeps a few settings on the device: the age group you chose (2–3 or 4–5), sound and voice on/off, the play time limit, the language, the number of stars earned and how many times each game was played. This information never leaves the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

If a parent enters it in the Parents' Corner, the app also keeps your child's first name or nickname (and how Chíp should say it) so Chíp can call your child by name. It stays only on the device, is never sent to us or anyone else, is not included in crash reports, and can be cleared at any time. If you leave it empty, Chíp simply says “sweetie”. Like your other settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or Google device backup.

4. Anonymous crash reports

If the app crashes, it can send a crash report through Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, so that we can find and fix the problem. A report contains technical details only: the error and where in the code it happened, the device model, operating system and app version, a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics, and a little app state (which game was open, the language, the age group, how many games are unlocked, and the last few screens visited).

Crash reports are not linked to any person, are not used for advertising or tracking, and are not combined with other data. Google stores them for up to 90 days. Firebase Analytics is not used. A parent can turn crash reports off at any time in the Parents' Corner › Crash reports. Learn more in Firebase's privacy information.

5. Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google) and always require a parent to pass the parent gate first. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we never see your name, payment card or account details. The app only learns which of its products the device owns, so it can unlock them.

6. Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and follows the Apple Kids Category guidelines, the Google Play Families policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because we do not collect personal information from children, there is no personal information to review or delete. Links out of the app and purchases sit behind a parent gate (hold a button for 2 seconds).

7. Third parties

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics. The only third-party service is Firebase Crashlytics, described above. Apple and Google process purchases.

8. Your choices

  • Turn crash reports off in the Parents' Corner.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on the device.
  • Contact us at classyapp.support@gmail.com with any question or request.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what information the app collects, we will also describe it in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
